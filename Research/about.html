More than coffee.

We're a community of roasters, farmers, and baristas who believe in ethical sourcing, exceptional flavour, and the power of a great cup.

Bloom & Bean Artisan Roastery was founded in 2021 in the heart of Maboneng, Johannesburg. What started as a weekend market stall — serving single-origin pour-overs to curious locals — quickly grew into a flagship cafe and roastery by 2024.

We work directly with smallholder farmers in Ethiopia and Rwanda, building long-term relationships that go beyond fair trade. Our beans are hand-picked, sun-dried, and shipped with full traceability.

Every roast is crafted in small batches to highlight the unique terroir of each origin. We believe coffee should be both delicious and just — for the farmer, the barista, and the drinker.

Mission

To make ethically sourced, freshly roasted coffee accessible while uplifting local farmers and baristas through education and fair wages.

Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted sustainable coffee brand by 2028 — setting the standard for transparency, quality, and community impact.
